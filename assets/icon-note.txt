icon.png is a placeholder (solid #7c6af7 square).

Before building the distributable:
  Mac: replace with icon.icns (512x512 + retina variants)
  Win: replace with icon.ico  (256x256 multi-resolution)

To generate from a 1024x1024 PNG:
  Mac: use iconutil or makeicns
       mkdir icon.iconset
       sips -z 512 512 icon.png --out icon.iconset/icon_512x512.png
       iconutil -c icns icon.iconset -o icon.icns
  Win: use ImageMagick:
       convert icon.png -resize 256x256 icon.ico
